## 🎯 Interview Preparation Strategy

For every problem, practice these **5 things**:

1. **Understand the problem**
2. **Explain your approach**
3. **Write the solution in JavaScript**
4. **Analyze Time & Space Complexity**
5. **Test with different examples**

> **Goal:** Solve all 30 problems without looking at the solution and explain your approach clearly during an interview.
